# Shuffled Caesar

**Author**: [Senhadji M Said](https://github.com/SenhadjiMSaid)


## Description:

We encrypted a secret message using a modified Caesar cipher. Instead of a simple shift, we shuffled the alphabet. Your task is to reverse the encryption and retrieve the flag.


## Hints:

1. The encryption involves a shuffled alphabet. Think of a Caesar cipher, but instead of shifting, we randomly rearranged the alphabet.
2. The random arrangement of the alphabet was generated using a fixed seed (`2025`), which makes the process reproducible.
3. Start by analyzing the frequency of characters in the encrypted text to deduce the mapping, or try to recreate the shuffled alphabet.


## File Provided:

- **flag.enc**: The encrypted flag.


## Goal:

Submit the decrypted flag in the format: `Alphabit{...}`.
